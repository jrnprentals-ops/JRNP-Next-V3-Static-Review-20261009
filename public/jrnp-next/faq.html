GUEST FAQ

Before you arrive.

What time is check-in and checkout?

Standard check-in begins at 4 PM, and checkout is by 10 AM. Instructions for your specific property are provided through your verified booking channel before arrival.

Can I request early check-in or late checkout?

Requests may be possible when cleaning and the next reservation permit. JRNP's current standard service fee is $50 for approved early check-in or late checkout. Please confirm availability and any charge with the host in advance; changes are not guaranteed.

Does checking a quote reserve dates?

No. A quote shows a current estimate and availability response, not a confirmed reservation or calendar hold. Direct requests must be reviewed and confirmed through JRNP's secure booking process.

Can I bring a pet or host a gathering?

Pets are not permitted unless your booking specifically authorizes them. Parties, smoking and unapproved gatherings are not permitted. Please review your property-specific rules before booking.

How will I enter the property?

Confirmed guests receive property-specific arrival and access instructions through their verified booking channel. Please do not use or share access codes from unrelated or older reservations.

What if I plan to arrive late at night?

If you expect to arrive after 10 PM, please confirm with JRNP so the team can be alert to your arrival. Check-in remains subject to the details of your confirmed stay.

Where can I park?

Parking arrangements differ among the Phoenix homes and the Weiss Lake property. Review the parking directions in your arrival guide; at some properties, specific spaces are reserved for the main house rather than the guest suite.

Is the Palm Haven pool heated? What about the spa?

At Palm Haven, the pool itself is not heated. Spa heating may be available by advance request; confirm notice requirements, fees and availability with JRNP before scheduling it.

What should I know about the Weiss Lake dock?

The Weiss Lake home features a private dock. Follow the on-site safety guidance around the lake, particularly after dark. Dock lighting is normally scheduled between 5 and 9 PM; do not assume lights or access arrangements beyond the confirmed guide.

Do you offer extended stays?

Longer stays may be available subject to property availability, deposit, rental agreement and scheduled cleaning. Extended stays normally require a lease or additional written terms, and biweekly cleaning may apply. Contact JRNP for a verified quotation.

Who can help if something needs attention during my stay?

Contact JRNP through the secure messaging channel tied to your confirmed booking. Include the property and a concise description, and notify the team promptly about any urgent maintenance or safety issue.
