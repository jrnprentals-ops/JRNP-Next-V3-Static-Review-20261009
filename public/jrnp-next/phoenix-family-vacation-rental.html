YOUR HOME AWAY FROM HOME

A place to settle in.

A four-bedroom Phoenix vacation home for gathering, relaxing and staying together.

Settle into a spacious Phoenix vacation rental designed for families and groups of up to eight guests. Four separate bedrooms give everyone room to unwind: a primary bedroom with a king bed and private en-suite bathroom, a second queen bedroom with its own en-suite bathroom, and two additional queen bedrooms served by the hall bathroom. With three bathrooms in all, the layout makes mornings and evenings easier for a full house.

The full kitchen is set up for meals at home, with a refrigerator, stove/cooking area, dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker, cookware and indoor dining space. Between outings, gather in the living room for a movie or board game, use the Wi-Fi and dedicated workspace, or take advantage of the washer and dryer for longer visits. Air conditioning and heating help keep the home comfortable across Phoenix seasons.

Outdoors, the private fenced backyard provides space to enjoy the desert evenings. A shaded covered patio, outdoor dining and seating, a propane grill, fire pit and lawn games make it easy to spend time together without needing another itinerary stop. Guest parking options include driveway space and street parking where permitted; review your arrival guide for instructions specific to your stay.

JRNP Rentals personally hosts this Phoenix family getaway. Check-in is from 4 PM and checkout is by 10 AM; access details follow an approved reservation. The home is for registered guests, with no smoking, parties or pets. Guests planning to arrive after 10 PM should tell the hosts in advance. BEFORE YOUR LAKE DAY

A lake that moves with the seasons.

Weiss Lake is an Alabama Power storage reservoir. Water levels rise and fall with rainfall, dam operations and seasonal flood management. The levels below are published seasonal operating-guide elevations—not today's measurements.

Typical summer guide564 ft MSL
Typical winter guide561 ft MSL

Alabama Power generally starts Weiss Lake's seasonal lowering in October and targets the winter operating level toward late November; exact timing and elevations may vary. The lake typically rises toward summer levels in spring.

Dock and boating note: Water depth, shoreline exposure and dock access can vary substantially. Photos may show different seasonal levels. Check current conditions and boat draft before launching or approaching the dock; do not assume water is deep enough for diving. Supervise children around the water. The boat lift is not included in guest use.
