BEFORE YOU BOOK

Good stays begin
with clarity.

Personally hosted, with care.

Understand JRNP's current general booking guidelines and important house expectations. Your final booking agreement and platform-specific reservation terms always govern your stay.

This page is a general guide for the new JRNP website, not a signed rental contract. Airbnb, Vrbo, Furnished Finder and direct-booking terms may differ. Verify all prices, dates, refunds, occupancy and services in your confirmed reservation before making a payment.

01 / BOOKING REQUESTS

Request-to-book.

Checking dates and seeing an estimate does not reserve a home. A direct booking is not confirmed until JRNP accepts the request and completes the applicable agreement and required payment steps.

Reservation confirmation and property access details will only be supplied through a trusted booking channel.

02 / DEPOSITS & BALANCE

Payment timing.

The current direct-booking working policy is a 50% initial deposit and the remaining balance seven days before arrival. For stays beginning within seven days, the full balance may be due when the booking is accepted. Final amounts, taxes, service fees and deadlines appear in the verified quote and agreement.

Do not submit deposits in response to unverified payment instructions or an availability estimate.

03 / CANCELLATIONS

Plans sometimes change.

JRNP's current direct-booking working guidelines provide a full refund for eligible cancellations at least 30 days before arrival, and a 50% refund for eligible cancellations at least 14 days before arrival. The precise calculation, exceptions, payment processing and refund eligibility are determined by the final accepted rental agreement.

If booked through Airbnb or Vrbo, review the cancellation policy shown on that platform for your reservation rather than assuming the direct-booking policy applies.

04 / ARRIVAL & DEPARTURE

Timing and access.

Standard check-in is 4 PM and checkout is 10 AM. An approved early check-in or late checkout may carry a $50 fee, subject to cleaning schedules and availability. Please notify JRNP if you expect to arrive after 10 PM.

Door codes and parking arrangements depend on the property and booking channel. Only your current confirmed arrival guide should be used.

05 / THE HOMES

Respect the space.

No parties, smoking or pets unless expressly permitted by your reservation. Occupancy limits and property-specific rules must be followed. Extra fees may apply for unauthorized pets, smoking, damages or unusual cleaning as set out in your agreement.

Guests must care for furnishings, follow safety instructions around pools and docks, and report maintenance or safety issues promptly.

06 / SPECIAL SERVICES

Property-specific details.

The Palm Haven pool is not heated; spa heating is available only by confirmed advance arrangement, with applicable notice and fees agreed in writing. The Weiss Lake property includes a private dock, with on-site safety guidance and normally scheduled dock lighting between 5 and 9 PM.

Long stays may require additional lease terms, a refundable deposit and scheduled cleaning. Ask JRNP to confirm the specific requirements for your property and dates.

07 / QUESTIONS

Let's make it clear.

Need details about a cancellation, availability, added guest, parking or a special request? Reach out before making a reservation change.
