FROM REQUEST TO THE FRONT DOOR

A clear path to check-in.

Private arrival information is released only after confirmation and property-readiness checks.

  1. 01

    Request reviewed

    JRNP verifies dates, guest details, special requests, pricing, and property fit. A request is not a confirmed reservation.

  2. 02

    Approval before payment

    If the request is approved, JRNP sends written next steps. No card is charged when a request is submitted.

  3. 03

    Reservation confirmed

    Your confirmation records the home, dates, guest count, booking reference, and payment status.

  4. 04

    Arrival preferences

    Use My Stay to share your timing, preferred contact method, and practical needs before arrival.

  5. 05

    Property readiness

    The host team verifies the home, parking guidance, guest entrance, and unique access details.

  6. 06

    Guided arrival

    Confirmed guests receive property-specific directions and access information at the appropriate time.

  7. 07

    Direct stay support

    Your reservation stays connected to the JRNP host team for practical help throughout the trip.

  8. 08

    Simple checkout

    Your reminder includes the home's verified checkout time and property-specific closing steps.
